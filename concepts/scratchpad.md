# Project Idea Scratchpad
List of potential project ideas I may or may not pursue

### DIY Pick and Place SMT Machine
A good project to use spare NEMA 17 stepper motors and automate surface-mount soldering
### Cleaning Robot for Tight Spaces (e.g. Swiffer pad sized)
A compact enough robot that can maneuver and clean hard floors in narrow spaces, like behind a couch, under a small table, etc.
### Snow Removal Robot for Sidewalks and Private Outdoor Spaces
Thinking of a robot that has mapped the perimeter of my house so it can melt snow (via boiling with a hot water egress at the bottom for safety?). Snow blowers are loud, only redirect snow and run on gasoline. This solution could also be less harmful alternative to rock salt.
