# Injection Molded Casio F-91W Case
## Purpose
Injection molding and tool design are manufacturing disciplines present in our everyday lives, but are not taught thoroughly in general engineering curriculum's. As an early adopter of FDM 3D printing, material science surrounding thermoplastics has always been a subject of interest. I wanted to attempt my first mold design at home using Onshape's free mold tools and test it using the LNS Technologies Model 300A. The goal is to apply these design principles to overmold or mold things from recycled plastics such as polypropylene (PP) and high-density polyethylene (HDPE).

## Design Requirements
The key constraints of this project are defined by the mechanical footprint of the Model 300A.
